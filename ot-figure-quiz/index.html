Step 2: everyone plays

There is no waiting room and no scoreboard. Each student gets their own result on their own phone. After everyone finishes, go around the room and ask who matched with whom, then ask why

Discussion ideas for the debrief

  • Who was surprised by their result, and which question do you think tipped it
  • Which figure did you hope to get, and what does that say about what you admire
  • Which of these people made a mistake, and what did God do next
