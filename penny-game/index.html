The Penny Problem

Everyone gets to choose one of three paths for the next 30 days:

  • Penny doubling: start with one penny, and it doubles every day
  • $1,500,000 flat: take the money today, no changes
  • 10% daily growth: start with $100,000, and it grows 10% every day

Before you reveal what happens, have each of your 15 students lock in a choice below.

#StudentChoiceResult

For discussion: All three paths start out looking unequal, and for most of the month the flat $1,500,000 looks like the smartest pick. The doubling penny quietly overtakes the 10% growth plan on Day 28, then passes the flat $1,500,000 on Day 29 to take the lead for good. The 10% growth plan does not catch up to the flat option until Day 30, the very last day. Small, steady, compounding choices can end up outpacing something that looks big and certain up front — a principle Doctrine and Covenants 64:33 teaches directly.
